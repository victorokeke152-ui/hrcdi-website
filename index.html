Frequently Asked Questions

What is HRCDI's exact CAC legal registration status?

HRCDI is filed under Part F (Incorporated Trustees) of the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA 2020) with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), Nigeria. It is currently completing its statutory 28-day public newspaper notice publication window maturing on November 2, 2026.

How can institutional partners request our organizational profile?

Donor bodies and partner organizations can contact our desk directly at info@hrcdi.org.ng to receive our formal profile and governance documentation.

How can I support or sponsor HRCDI community programs?

Individual supporters, sponsors, and grant bodies can get in touch with our team directly via email at info@hrcdi.org.ng to discuss partnership frameworks and official sponsorship channels.
