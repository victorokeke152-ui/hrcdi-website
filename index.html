Standard Governance & Operational Policy

1. Institutional Mandate & Governance

  • 1.1 Non-Profit Status: Hope Rising Community Development Initiative (HRCDI) is constituted as an independent, non-governmental, non-profit organization operating in Maiduguri, Borno State, and across Nigeria.
  • 1.2 Statutory Compliance: All operations, filings, and community initiatives shall align with statutory requirements set forth by relevant administrative and regulatory bodies.
  • 1.3 Governance Authority: The Board of Trustees (BoT) and Executive Leadership maintain overriding authority over policy updates, project selection, and structural appointments.

2. Financial Governance & Grant Management

  • 2.1 Institutional Banking: All grants, donations, and project contributions must be received exclusively through corporate bank accounts registered in the official name of the organization.
  • 2.2 Dual Authorization: Capital disbursements exceeding operational thresholds require joint approval from designated Board Trustees and Executive Leadership.
  • 2.3 Fiscal Transparency: Full accounting records, receipts, and audit logs shall be retained for all community welfare distributions, educational aid, and outreach expenditures.

3. Ethical Field Operations & Code of Conduct

  • 3.1 Neutrality & Non-Discrimination: HRCDI maintains absolute political, religious, and tribal neutrality. All welfare distribution and community assistance are rendered strictly based on identified need.
  • 3.2 Beneficiary Protection: Field personnel must treat all program beneficiaries, including youth, widows, and vulnerable households, with absolute dignity, respect, and fairness.
  • 3.3 Zero Tolerance Policy: Any form of exploitation, harassment, or unauthorized solicitation of funds by staff or field representatives will result in immediate termination of appointment and legal escalation where applicable.

4. Staff Authorization & Data Privacy

  • 4.1 Official Verification: All personnel representing HRCDI in official capacities must possess a valid, verified record within the HRCDI Staff Portal.
  • 4.2 Data Confidentiality: Personal identification numbers (NIN), contact details, and field data collected from beneficiaries, staff, or donors shall be handled securely and processed exclusively for legitimate institutional purposes.

STANDARD ORGANIZATIONAL GOVERNANCE & OPERATIONAL POLICY

REF: HRCDI/POL/2026/01 • EFFECTIVE DATE: OCTOBER 2026

1. Institutional Mandate & Governance

Hope Rising Community Development Initiative (HRCDI) is constituted as an independent, non-governmental organization in Borno State. All assets, donations, and statutory contributions acquired by HRCDI are dedicated solely toward fulfilling its non-profit community welfare, education, healthcare, and humanitarian objectives under trustee oversight.

2. Financial Governance & Grant Management

All grants, donations, and financial contributions must be processed exclusively through institutional bank accounts registered in the official name of the organization. Capital disbursements require dual trustee authorization and full fiscal accounting logs.

3. Ethical Field Operations & Code of Conduct

HRCDI maintains complete political, religious, and tribal neutrality. All welfare distributions, youth programs, and healthcare projects are rendered strictly based on community need. Exploitation, harassment, or unauthorized fee collection by field staff will result in immediate termination.

4. Staff Authorization & Data Privacy

All personnel representing HRCDI in official capacities must possess a valid, verified record within the HRCDI Staff Portal. Personal details collected from program beneficiaries, staff, or donors are handled securely and solely for statutory and institutional compliance.
