STANDARD ORGANIZATIONAL GOVERNANCE & OPERATIONAL POLICY

REF: HRCDI/POL/2026/01 • EFFECTIVE DATE: OCTOBER 2026

1. INSTITUTIONAL MANDATE & GOVERNANCE

Hope Rising Community Development Initiative (HRCDI) is constituted as an independent, non-governmental organization under CAMA 2020 framework. All assets, donations, and statutory contributions acquired by HRCDI are dedicated solely toward fulfilling its non-profit community welfare, education, healthcare, and humanitarian objectives under trustee oversight.

2. FINANCIAL DISBURSEMENT & CONTROLS

All grants, donations, and financial contributions must be processed exclusively through institutional bank accounts registered in the official name of the organization. Capital disbursements require dual trustee authorization and full fiscal accounting logs.

3. ETHICAL FIELD OPERATIONS & CODE OF CONDUCT

HRCDI maintains complete political, religious, and tribal neutrality. All welfare distributions, youth programs, and healthcare projects are rendered strictly based on community need. Exploitation, harassment, or unauthorized fee collection by field staff will result in immediate termination.

4. STAFF AUTHORIZATION & DATA PRIVACY

All personnel representing HRCDI in official capacities must possess a valid, verified record within the HRCDI Staff Portal. Personal details collected from program beneficiaries, staff, or donors are handled securely and solely for statutory and institutional compliance.
