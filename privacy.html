Privacy Policy

How Hope Rising Community Development Initiative protects and manages your personal data.

1. Overview

Hope Rising Community Development Initiative ("HRCDI", "we", "us", or "our") respects your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website at hrcdi.org.ng or engage with our community programs, donations, and volunteer outreach.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you contact us, make a donation, or register as a volunteer, including:

  • Full Name
  • Email address (info@hrcdi.org.ng communications)
  • Phone number
  • Mailing address
  • Donation reference details (we do not store full payment card details)

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to support our non-profit mission, specifically to:

  • Process donations and issue receipts.
  • Respond to general inquiries and offer project updates.
  • Coordinate volunteer and community outreach programs in Borno State and across Nigeria.
  • Comply with regulatory obligations under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) standards.

4. Data Protection & Sharing

We implement appropriate security measures to safeguard your personal data. We never sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only shared when required by law or to facilitate authorized banking transactions for donations.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data removal, please reach out to us at:

Hope Rising Community Development Initiative (HRCDI)

Maiduguri, Borno State, Nigeria

Email: info@hrcdi.org.ng
