About HRCDI

Dedicated to sustainable community empowerment, healthcare intervention, and youth advocacy across Borno State, Nigeria.

Our Mission

To uplift vulnerable rural and urban populations in Nigeria through structured social infrastructure, vocational capacity building, healthcare relief, and ethical community leadership.

Our Vision

A resilient Nigerian society where underserved communities have equal access to economic self-reliance, basic healthcare support, and dignified living conditions.

Legal & Governance Framework

HRCDI operates as an Incorporated Trustee under Part F of the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA 2020), committed to maximum financial accountability, safeguarding protocols, and regulatory compliance.
