Institutional Governance & Compliance

Staff Code of Conduct & Operational Ethics

Legally binding operational standards, safeguarding mandates, and ethical standards required for all personnel, executives, and representatives of Hope Rising Community Development Initiative (HRCDI).

Mandatory Staff & Volunteer Agreement

All employees, contractors, and field representatives must review, sign, and adhere strictly to this policy upon onboarding.

1. Scope & Obligation

This Code of Conduct applies to all full-time, part-time, project-based, and contract staff employed by Hope Rising Community Development Initiative (HRCDI). Adherence to this Code is a mandatory condition of employment. Failure to abide by these standards will lead to formal disciplinary action.

2. Safeguarding & Beneficiary Protection (Zero Tolerance)

  • Protection Against Sexual Exploitation & Abuse (PSEA): HRCDI enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding sexual exploitation, abuse, or workplace harassment (SEAH).
  • Protection of Vulnerable Groups: All staff must prioritize the safety, dignity, and standard of care for children and vulnerable community members across Borno State field operations.
  • Prohibition of Power Exploitation: Personnel must never exchange aid, materials, or services for personal favors, labor, sexual favors, or financial gains.

3. Anti-Bribery, Asset Management & Financial Integrity

  • Anti-Corruption: Staff are strictly prohibited from soliciting, offering, or accepting bribes, kickbacks, or gifts that could influence program decisions or procurement.
  • Use of Institutional Property: HRCDI resources, official domain emails (@hrcdi.org.ng), vehicles, and equipment must be used strictly for authorized operations.
  • Conflict of Interest: Personnel must immediately disclose any personal or business affiliations that present a potential conflict of interest.

4. Confidential Whistleblower Channel

HRCDI maintains a confidential reporting channel managed directly by the Board of Trustees. Any staff member, volunteer, or community beneficiary who witnesses ethical breaches, harassment, or financial misconduct can submit a confidential report without fear of retaliation.
